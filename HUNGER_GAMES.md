# Hunger Games — Player Guide

This is a fast, combat-focused way to play LOTMCraft: instead of the normal slow Beyonder progression grind, you level up (advance sequence) primarily by **fighting and killing other players**, plus a couple of special items. Here's what you need to know.

## The two special items

- **Sequence Advancement Item** — use it while you're sequence 2 or lower... actually, use it at sequence ≥ 2 (i.e. you're not already at the top) and it **instantly** advances you one sequence on your current pathway. No potion-drinking animation, no risk of failure — it just works, and your digestion is topped off to 100% in the process. Consumed on use.
- **Characteristic Essence Item** — use it at any sequence ≥ 1 to instantly grant yourself a characteristic stack for your current sequence. Consumed on use.

Both are craftable/obtainable as normal items — grab them when you find them, since they're a guaranteed, no-risk way to progress.

## Kill someone → advance instantly

If you kill another Beyonder player **and your own digestion is already full** (from the combat drain above), you **instantly advance one sequence** the moment they die — no potion, no roll, no animation. Your digestion then resets to 0 so you have to build it back up again before your next kill can trigger another advance.

**Cooldown: this can only trigger once every 7 minutes per player.** Chain-killing back to back won't stack free advancements — you'll need to wait out the cooldown even if your digestion fills again.

## 

Drinking a Beyonder potion to advance still works exactly like normal — same success/failure odds based on your sanity, same pathway-switch restrictions, same risk of dying on a bad roll — but it now resolves **immediately**. There's no floating/fog/camera animation anymore; you drink it and find out right away.

Digestion no longer affects your odds here — only sanity does. Practically:
- If you're advancing to a sequence only 1 level up (or staying on the same sequence) and your sanity is ≥ 80%, you have a guaranteed success.
- Advancing 2 sequences at once is still risky (90% base failure) regardless of digestion.

## Getting your first sequence is much easier

You can directly jump to sequence 5.

## Other things to know

- You won't need to worry about hunger/food while fighting — Beyonders get free saturation regardless of sequence.
- Loot chests around the map may or may not contain bonus Beyonder potions/ingredients/characteristics — this is still being tuned, so don't rely on it.
- There's no built-in match structure (no shrinking border, no forced teams, no automatic spectating on death) — treat those as whatever your server/host sets up separately.

## Quick summary

| How to advance | What happens |
|---|---|
| Use a **Sequence Advancement Item** | Instant +1 sequence, no risk |
| Use a **Characteristic Essence Item** | Instant characteristic stack, no risk |
| **Kill a player** with digestion already full | Instant +1 sequence, then 7-minute cooldown before it can trigger again |
| Drink a potion at sequence ≥ 5 (first time advancing) | Guaranteed success, resolves instantly |
| Drink a potion otherwise | Normal sanity-based odds, resolves instantly, digestion doesn't matter |
